KIONI SKIN CASE STUDY
                        ABOUT THE BRAND
                        Founder, Maria Mandroules, an ex-practicing
                        Emergency Nurse Practitioner having graduated from
                        the University of Sydney, Cumberland College of
                        Health Sciences, the University of Newcastle and
                        Central Queensland University.

                        She is also a single mum of 4, and it is when 2 of
                        her children experienced the debilitating flare-ups
                        of eczema that she used her scientific and research
                        knowledge to look for an answer that supported
                        and nurtured the skin barrier with the hopes of
                        managing their eczema with minimal flare-ups and
                        potentially minimal use of steroids.




KIONI SKIN CASE STUDY
                        ABOUT THE PRODUCT
                        That is when she came across the exciting
                        world of the microbiome and the gut-
                        skin-brain axis. The skin microbiome
                        is largely part of this axis, and
                        skin inflammation is influenced by any
                        disturbances in these areas.

                        So, she thought, if there is a two-
                        way communication between these axes,
                        and people have been taking probiotics
                        for gut health for years, would there
                        be enhanced skin health benefits if
                        probiotics were added directly to
                        skincare?




KIONI SKIN CASE STUDY
                        WHY KIONI SKIN?
                        In keeping with her Greek island heritage,
                        Maria chose the name Kioni. This is the name
                        of a small village on the Greek island of
                        Ikaria- one of the worlds 5 Blue Zones. It
                        is here that people live well into their
                        90’s and over 100, almost completely free
                        from dementia and other chronic diseases.
                        They prioritise a healthy diet, socialising
                        and living a balanced life.

                        These are the same principles that Kioni
                        Skin believes is required for a balanced,
                        nurtured, healthy skin microbiome.

KIONI SKIN CASE STUDY
                        COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
                        Noteworthy competitor brands that saturate
                        the market, predominantly exhibit a
                        clinical and commercial aesthetic. Scientific
                        formulations and medical endorsements are
                        mosty prioritised, resulting in packaging
                        and branding that feel sterile and
                        impersonal.

                        While these products may be similar in
                        function to Kioni's, their branding is
                        void of the nurturing, holistic ethos that
                        Kioni strives for. Instead, they emphasize
                        clinical efficacy over emotional connection,
                        which may alienate consumers seeking a more
                        personal and relatable skincare experience.
