Studio

Good people,
good things.

A small creative studio on the Gold Coast. Ten years in, still no account layer.

Good Apples started in 2016. We rebranded in 2018, then again in 2024, which tells you we are willing to take our own advice. The studio is owned and operated by PeriscoPe, so when a job needs more hands than we have, it gets them, and when it doesn't, nobody pretends otherwise.

We are not the biggest studio on the Gold Coast and we have never tried to be. We are a small group of skilled operators who would rather do six things properly than sixty at arm's length. The person you brief is the person making the work. There is no account layer in between, no deck built to justify an invoice, and no bad humans on our side of the table, or yours.

We work with brands in wellness, food, sport and the outdoors, mostly because we actually use the stuff. It turns out caring about the product makes the work better. Who knew.

How it actually runs

Most jobs start with a conversation, not a questionnaire. We would rather understand the business than collect a brief, so expect questions about margins and stockists alongside the ones about colour.

From there the work is made in the open. You see it early, while it is still cheap to change, and you see the reasoning with it. We do not save it all up for a reveal, because a reveal is a performance and performances are expensive.

Then we hand over properly. Files your team can open, systems they can use without ringing us, and a brand that still holds up three years and forty touchpoints later. If you want us to keep running it, we are here. If you don't, nothing breaks when we leave.
