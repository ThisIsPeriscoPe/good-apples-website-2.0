Veteran Games

A year of the Veteran Games, from VeteranGames.com to the stadium screens.

The Veteran Games is Australia's premier military-inspired team competition for Veterans. It is a proactive health initiative, created to address key findings from the Royal Commission into Defence and Veteran Suicide. The competition is the vehicle. Connection and purpose are the point.

2026 ran four events across two countries: the Gold Coast Heats, the UK Expedition Event, the Darwin Heats and the 2026 Finals at Sunshine Coast Stadium, where 11 Teams from Australia, the UK and the USA competed. We built the new VeteranGames.com, with a live stream hub, event recaps and forms that feed straight into email, and wrote and sent 35 emails across the year.

For the Finals we made 18 stadium screen sequences, 12 team intro films, 21 sponsor lower thirds and 31 awards and presenter graphics for the live stream, plus 12 billboards and the stage sponsor wall. Finals weekend drew 376K Instagram views and 28 times the website visitors of the weekend before.
