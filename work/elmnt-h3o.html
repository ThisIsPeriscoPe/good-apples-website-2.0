Work/ELMNT H3O

ELMNT H3O

Launching hydrogen water, from the first can to the first sale.

ELMNT set out to make drinks that lift everyday wellness, and launched with H3O, a hydrogen-infused water. We came in at the very start in May 2024, setting up the launch basics: domains, email, business cards and a landing page feeding Klaviyo.

Then the big pieces. We planned and art directed a full-day launch shoot across Currumbin and Burleigh, with beach yoga, swimming and running; designed and built the Shopify site; and created the packaging: 500 mL cans in four flavours, tablet boxes, blister packs and shipper cartons.

Trade marketing print, a sales deck and a brand deck on hydrogen-rich water rounded out the launch.
