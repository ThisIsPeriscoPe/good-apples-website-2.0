Sprout

The first real change to infant formula in 120 years.

In June 2021 Sprout launched its flagship product: a plant based organic infant formula, approved by leading paediatricians, dietitians and food science experts, in a category that had seen almost no change in more than 120 years.

Another brand we built from the ground up, starting here in Australia. We crafted the identity, designed the packaging, and grew it to where it is today.
