Work/Pharmacy Guild

Pharmacy Guild

Pharmacy has evolved. We had sixty seconds to show it.

The Pharmacy Guild of Australia has represented community pharmacy for almost 100 years. Its quality program, QCPP, is evolving into the Quality and Safety Pharmacy Program, QSPP, shifting the emphasis from compliance alone to quality and safety in everyday practice.

Akin Agency brought us in to tell that story. From Akin's script and voiceover we illustrated and animated “Pharmacy has Evolved”, a 60-second 2D explainer that runs as one continuous timeline, from the counter of a 1997 pharmacy to consult rooms, vaccinations and the modern streetscape. It was built to loop on the Guild's stall at the APP Conference, with web cuts, a screensaver loop and three social cuts made from the master.

Then the Guild came to us directly for a second explainer: sixty seconds of tanks, taps and scripts showing how the 8th Community Pharmacy Agreement delivers its full $2.111 billion ACSS commitment to community pharmacy.

What we did

  • Web and Social Cutdowns
