BikeChain

Fifteen hooks, five ads, one look.

BikeChain is an Australian online cycling retailer for apparel, parts, accessories and nutrition, and every order supports a local bike shop the customer picks. Working with PeriscoPe's paid social team, we design and write its Meta ads.

The first batch took five proven ads and gave each three new hooks: fifteen ads, written and designed, delivered by 3pm so they could be approved the same day. Every image got the same finish, a grade, a slow-shutter motion streak, halation and film grain, so the whole set looks like one artist made it.

For Black Friday we made a static for each of seven brands, Alpinestars, Northwave, Santini, The Vandal, Zone3, BBB Cycling and Hamax, in three sizes each.
