ICONIC Beverages

A founder's ring, kept, sharpened and ready for the big leagues.

ICONIC Beverages calls itself Australia's premium, independent national spirits distributor, with brands like Tito's Handmade Vodka, WhistlePig and Papa Salt Coastal Gin in its portfolio. The brief was to evolve the identity, not reinvent it: more premium and professional in front of global brand partners and big wholesalers, with no cursive, no boxed badge, no illustration and nothing turned into a cocktail glass.

The founder's ring has been part of ICONIC since day one, and the buyers, venue managers and partners they work with already know it. So we kept it, gave it a heavier weight and a set of metal gradients, and paired it with a new wordmark. Three rounds later ICONIC signed off a full suite: four lockups in nine colourways, a colour and type system, and rules for co-branding with partners.

It all lives in a brand guidelines website and a 32-page PDF, with an animated gold aura that loops on screens and in email.
