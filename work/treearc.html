TreeArc

A new name for a family office, drawn from a three-point arc.

TreeArc is an Australian family office investing for impact across climate, technology and community. It started life as Wollemi Capital Group, and after six years it needed a name and an identity that matched where it was heading.

We visualised the shortlisted names so the family could see them, not just read them. TreeArc won, and the mark was drawn to feel less like an arrow and more like a basketball three-point arc: three curves for the three guiding voices of the family, converging into one line, with a T and an A hidden in the overlap.

From there we built the guidelines, designed the coming-soon page and the new website, and took the brand into the office with decals, signage, a media wall and a pull-up banner.
