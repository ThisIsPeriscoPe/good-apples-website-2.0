Work/Zesty Paws

Zesty Paws

Emails, ads and pages for a pet brand that's always on.

Zesty Paws makes scientifically formulated supplements for dogs and cats, made in Australia. Through PeriscoPe, we handle the creative for its emails, ads and pages.

Since September 2026 that has meant three redesign routes for the Spring Sale email, sample-sachet offer ads, a mobile-first Hip and Joint introduction page built for cold paid-social traffic, with an A/B hero test and a jar picker matched to dog size, and 35 full-price Meta ad files cut from creator footage with new three-second hooks.

For Black Friday and Christmas we wrote and designed eight emails, built them as Klaviyo templates and a scheduled campaign, and made eight homepage hero banners for zestypaws.com.au.
