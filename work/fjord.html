Fjord

Cold therapy, built in Burleigh, now with a site to match.

Fjord builds world-class cold therapy products, ice baths, ice showers and chillers, for wellness spaces, gyms and premium home setups. It was founded by Danish-Australian brothers Nick and Dan, and every unit is hand-built in Southeast Queensland.

We redesigned the website from the ground up and built it on Shopify with PeriscoPe's developers, staged around the core experience: the ice bath range, ice showers, consumables, Our Work, the Journal and a builder for exploring colours, finishes and design options.

Around the launch we design and build Fjord's HubSpot emails, cut social ads and animated GIF ads for the paid team, and made the 2026 apparel range: heavy faded tees, a tote and a cap.
